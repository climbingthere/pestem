Our Method

Smarter Pest Control, Backed by Science

Most pest control companies show up on a fixed schedule — every month, same spray, same routine, whether pests are active or not. At Pest ’Em, we do things differently. We practice Integrated Pest Management (IPM), which means we start by identifying exactly what pest you’re dealing with and understanding how it lives, feeds, and reproduces. From there, we work through the most responsible solutions first: sealing entry points, removing the conditions pests need to thrive, and using targeted treatments only where they’re needed. Broad-spectrum pesticide applications are a last resort, not a first instinct.

We also time our treatments using a method called Growing Degree Days — GDD for short. Instead of guessing when pests will show up based on the calendar, we track accumulated heat data throughout the season to predict exactly when specific pest populations are hatching, emerging, or hitting their peak. Pest biology runs on temperature, not dates, and a warm March can shift everything by weeks. By monitoring GDD thresholds for each pest we manage, we treat during the narrow window when it will do the most good. That means fewer applications, less product in your environment, and better results than the old “spray and pray” approach.
