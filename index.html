What We Treat

Every Pest That Hits Warren County

Professional-grade treatment for the pests that actually show up in Blairstown. This is targeted, seasonal pest control, not a generic spray-and-leave service.

April - October
Tick Control

Monthly bifenthrin barrier spray along lawn edges, wooded borders, and shrub beds. Lyme disease is serious in Warren County, so we treat the zones where ticks actually live.

May - September
Mosquito Control

Barrier spray targeting shrubs and shaded vegetation where mosquitoes rest. Monthly treatments keep your yard usable all season long.

Year-Round
Rodent Prevention

Exterior bait stations, perimeter treatment, and monthly interior inspections in winter. Wooded lots mean constant pressure, and we stay ahead of it.

August - November
Stink Bug Control

Exterior seal treatment on window frames, doors, soffits, and siding gaps before they migrate inside for winter. Stop them at the wall.

May - November
Spotted Lanternfly

Vegetation and surface treatment for this invasive species that's destroying NJ's trees, vines, and gardens. Egg mass identification included.

Spring - Fall
Wasp & Hornet Removal

Safe nest removal from eaves, decks, and sheds. Treatment prevents them from rebuilding in the same spot. Same-week service available.
