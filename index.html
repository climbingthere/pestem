Mosquito Control

Seasonal Breakdown

The Right Treatment at the Right Time

Your flat monthly rate covers different work depending on the season — because what your property needs in July is not what it needs in January.

Exterior Season

April - October (7 visits)
  • Monthly exterior barrier spray around full perimeter
  • Bifenthrin-based perimeter treatment — 3 ft up foundation, 3 ft out on lawn edge
  • Targeted mosquito barrier in shrubs and vegetation (resting sites)
  • Tick control along lawn perimeter and wooded edges
  • Wasp and hornet nest prevention and removal
  • Spotted lanternfly surface and vegetation treatment

Interior Season

November - March (5 visits)
  • Monthly interior visit, same flat rate
  • Rodent bait station inspection and refill (Protecta LP stations)
  • Crack and crevice spray along baseboards, entry points, plumbing gaps
  • Spider treatment in basement, garage, utility areas
  • Stink bug interior treatment (overwintering in walls and attic)
  • Foundation exterior inspection — seal recommendations, moisture assessment
