What We Treat

Real Treatments. Every Season.

We treat what's actually active each month — not the same spray year-round regardless of what's living in your yard.

Tick Control

Blairstown sits at the edge of farmland and forest — prime black-legged tick habitat. Lyme disease, anaplasmosis, and babesiosis are real risks here. We create a monthly barrier so ticks never get to your family or your dogs.

  • Bifenthrin (Bifen IT) barrier spray — 30-day residual
  • 3-foot treatment band at lawn edges and wooded borders
  • Shrub beds, leaf litter, fence lines, stone walls
  • Monthly applications April through October

Mosquito Control

Mosquitoes rest in shade during the day — shrubs, dense vegetation, and wooded edges. We target those resting sites with a contact barrier spray that knocks down populations at the source. Your backyard cookouts, evenings on the porch, and kids playing outside stay comfortable all summer.

  • Barrier spray targets resting sites, not open lawn
  • Applied before peak activity hours for best coverage
  • Monthly May through September
  • Safe for pollinators — we avoid flowering plants
Learn about mosquito control

Stink Bug & Lanternfly Control

Stink bugs seek warmth inside NJ homes every fall. Spotted lanternflies are devastating Warren County trees and gardens as an invasive species. We treat both on the exterior before they become indoor problems — exterior seal, perimeter spray, and lanternfly vegetation treatment.

  • Cypermethrin exterior seal on windows, doors, soffits
  • Dinotefuran vegetation treatment for lanternflies
  • Egg mass identification and removal
  • Stink bug season: August–November

Rodent Prevention & Wasp Removal

Wooded Warren County lots mean constant mouse pressure — especially when temperatures drop. We install Protecta LP bait stations, treat the perimeter, and inspect monthly through winter. Wasp nests in eaves, decks, and sheds are removed on-site and treated to prevent rebuilding.

  • Protecta LP bait station installation and monthly inspection
  • Interior crack and crevice spray — baseboards, plumbing gaps
  • Wasp and hornet nest removal same-visit
  • Prevention treatment to stop nest rebuilding
