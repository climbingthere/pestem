Tick Control

Monthly bifenthrin barrier spray along lawn edges, wooded borders, and shrub beds. Lyme disease is serious in Warren County — we treat the zones where ticks actually live.

May – September
Mosquito Control

Barrier spray targeting shrubs and shaded vegetation where mosquitoes rest. Monthly treatments keep your yard usable all season long.

Year-Round
Rodent Prevention

Exterior bait stations, perimeter treatment, and monthly interior inspections in winter. Wooded lots mean constant pressure — we stay ahead of it.
