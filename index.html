Pest control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Warren County, NJ?

Quarterly plans run a flat per-visit rate. Your exact number comes from a free inspection, with no contracts and no auto-renew. First visits take longer and cost a bit more than regular visits. The fastest answer is a call: (888) 774-4290.

How fast can you come out?

Most first visits happen within the week, often sooner. Call (888) 774-4290 for the soonest opening.

What happens on the first visit?

Plan on 45 to 60 minutes. We start with a walkaround inspection, then treat where the evidence points: eaves and webs, cracks and crevices, the exterior barrier, the granular perimeter, and interior spots as needed. Every visit ends with a report and re-entry review, where we walk you through what we did, including any re-entry precautions for children and pets. A brief uptick in activity for a few days after treatment is normal, then it drops off. If anything is still moving, the re-treatment is free.

Do I need to leave the house during treatment? What about kids and pets?

Usually not for exterior work. We use EPA-registered products, applied strictly according to label directions, and we review every treatment with you before we leave, including any re-entry precautions for children and pets.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. The plan is quarterly and you can cancel anytime. There are no auto-renew traps. We'd rather earn the next visit than lock you into it.

Which towns do you serve?

All of Warren County: Blairstown, Hackettstown, Washington, Belvidere, Hope, Hardwick, Frelinghuysen, Knowlton, Oxford, and the surrounding towns, plus nearby parts of Sussex County. Not sure if you're in range? Call (888) 774-4290 and ask.
