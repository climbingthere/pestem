Pest control FAQs

How much does pest control cost in Warren County, NJ?

Quarterly prevention plans run a flat per-visit rate, and your exact number comes from a free inspection — no contracts, no auto-renew, cancel anytime. First visits take a little longer and price a little higher than regular visits. Fastest answer: call (888) 774-4290 and the owner will quote you over the phone.

What happens on the first visit?

Plan on 45–60 minutes. We start with the Walkaround Inspection, then treat where the evidence points: eaves and webs, cracks and crevices, the exterior barrier, granular perimeter, and interior spots as needed. Every visit ends with the Report & Re-Entry Review — we walk you through what we did, including any re-entry precautions for children and pets. It's normal to see a brief uptick in activity for a few days after treatment, then a steady decline. If anything's still moving, the re-treatment is free.

What is growing-degree-day (GDD) pest timing?

Insects develop on accumulated heat, not calendar dates. Growing degree days measure that heat, adding up day by day from January 1. Every pest wakes up near a known GDD threshold — carpenter ant swarmers around 100–400, lanternfly hatch at 150–200, yellowjacket nests at 600–800. We track Blairstown's GDD all season and time each visit to land inside those windows, which is something a fixed monthly route calendar simply can't do.

Do I need to leave the house during treatment? What about kids and pets?

Usually not for exterior work. We use EPA-registered products, applied strictly according to label directions, and we review every treatment with you — including any re-entry precautions for children and pets — before we leave.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. The plan is quarterly, you can cancel anytime, and there are no auto-renew traps. We'd rather earn the next visit than lock you into it.

Do you treat spotted lanternfly on trees?

We treat lanternfly egg masses, nymphs, and adult swarms on your home, deck, and exterior perimeter. Spraying trees and orchards requires a different NJ DEP license category, so we don't offer it — and we're happy to point you to a provider who does.

Do you handle termites or mosquitoes?

No — and we'll tell you that up front. We're licensed for general and household pest control (NJ DEP Category 7A). Termite contracts and mosquito treatments require different license categories, and if you need them we'll gladly tell you who to call.

Which towns do you serve?

Blairstown, Hope, Hardwick, Frelinghuysen, Knowlton, Hackettstown, Newton, and Belvidere — plus nearby Warren and Sussex County towns. Not sure if you're in range? Call (888) 774-4290 and ask.
