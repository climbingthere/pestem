Tick Control

Warren County sits in one of the highest-risk zones for Lyme disease in the country. Our tick control program uses targeted perimeter sprays and granular treatments in shaded, wooded edge areas where ticks thrive. We treat every 3-4 weeks from April through October, with an initial intensive application to knock down existing populations fast. Safe for pets once dry (typically 30 minutes).

Full Details
Quick Facts
  • Season: April - October
  • Frequency: Every 3-4 weeks
  • Pet Safe: Yes, once dry
  • Included in: Standard & Full plans

Mosquito Control

Our mosquito program combines barrier spray treatments on foliage, fences, and shaded resting areas with larvicide applications to standing water sources. This two-pronged approach reduces adult mosquito populations by up to 90%. Treatments run every 3 weeks from May through September, so you can enjoy your yard all summer long.

Full Details
Quick Facts
  • Season: May - September
  • Frequency: Every 3 weeks
  • Pet Safe: Yes, once dry
  • Included in: Standard & Full plans

Rodent Prevention

Mice and rats are not just a nuisance; they chew wiring, contaminate food, and carry disease. Our rodent program starts with a thorough inspection to find entry points, followed by exclusion sealing with steel wool and caulk. We place tamper-resistant bait stations around your home's exterior and monitor them quarterly. Prevention is always better than eviction.

Quick Facts
  • Season: Year-round (peak: fall/winter)
  • Frequency: Quarterly monitoring
  • Includes: Entry point sealing
  • Included in: Full Protection plan

Stink Bug Control

Brown marmorated stink bugs swarm Warren County homes every fall, looking for warm spots to overwinter. Once they are inside, they are nearly impossible to deal with without creating a mess. Our exterior barrier treatments in late August and September create a chemical perimeter that stops them before they enter. We treat window frames, door frames, eaves, and soffits.

Quick Facts
  • Season: August - October
  • Frequency: 2-3 treatments
  • Method: Exterior barrier spray
  • Included in: Full Protection plan

Spotted Lanternfly Treatment

Spotted lanternflies have arrived in Warren County and they damage trees, plants, and outdoor surfaces with sticky honeydew. Our treatment targets nymphs and adults on tree trunks, hardscape, and plants. We use systemic treatments for high-value trees and contact sprays for active infestations. Treatments are most effective from May through October.

Quick Facts
  • Season: May - October
  • Frequency: As needed
  • Method: Systemic + contact spray
  • Included in: Full Protection plan

Ants & Spiders

Carpenter ants, pavement ants, and various spider species are common in Warren County homes. Our perimeter treatment creates a barrier around your foundation, entry points, and common nesting areas. Interior treatments are available when needed. Quarterly applications keep populations suppressed year-round. We identify the species first so we use the right approach.

Quick Facts
  • Season: Year-round (peak: spring/summer)
  • Frequency: Quarterly
  • Method: Perimeter + interior
  • Included in: All plans
