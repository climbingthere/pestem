Our Services

Comprehensive pest control for every threat in Warren County. Prevention-first treatments that keep your home protected year-round.

Tick Control

Warren County's wooded properties make tick exposure a year-round concern. Our tick program targets the spots where ticks actually live -- property perimeters, leaf litter, stone walls, and wooded edges. We use EPA-approved products applied at the right times to break the tick lifecycle.

Lyme disease is endemic in our area, and the deer population here keeps tick numbers high. Our barrier treatments create a protective zone around your yard that dramatically cuts tick populations and keeps your family and pets safer.

Mosquito Control

Take back your yard with our monthly barrier spray program. We treat foliage, shaded areas, and standing water zones where mosquitoes rest and breed. The residual barrier kills mosquitoes on contact and repels new ones from moving in.

Our mosquito program runs May through October -- the full NJ mosquito season. Each treatment lasts about 21 days, and we time visits to maintain continuous protection. We also identify breeding sites like clogged gutters, birdbaths, and low spots that hold water.

Rodent Prevention

Mice and rats don't just appear -- they find entry points that have been there all along. We start with a thorough inspection of your home's exterior, finding every gap, crack, and opening (even ones as small as a dime) that rodents can use to get in.

We seal entry points with professional-grade materials, install monitoring stations around the perimeter, and set up exclusion barriers at high-risk spots like garage doors, foundation vents, and utility penetrations. Our approach stops rodents before they get inside.

Stink Bug Control

Every fall, Warren County homes get hit with brown marmorated stink bugs looking for warm shelter. They cluster around windows, door frames, and siding in huge numbers. Once they're inside, getting them out without the smell is nearly impossible.

We take a preventative approach. We apply exterior barrier treatments in late August and September, before the bugs start moving indoors. We hit windows, doors, eaves, soffits, and every crack in the building envelope. Timing is everything -- treat too late and they're already in your walls.

Spotted Lanternfly Control

Spotted lanternflies have spread across New Jersey fast, damaging trees, crops, and making outdoor spaces sticky and unpleasant. They feed on tree sap and excrete honeydew that promotes mold and attracts other insects.

We use systemic and contact treatments on tree trunks, tree-of-heaven stands, and other host plants. We target lanternflies at multiple life stages -- from egg masses in spring to adults in summer and fall. We also find and destroy egg masses during inspections.

Ants, Spiders & General Pest

A few ants in the kitchen usually means thousands more hidden in your walls or foundation. We find and treat colonies directly with targeted baits and residual products that workers carry back to the nest. No surface-level spraying that just moves the problem around.

For spiders, we focus on interior web removal and exterior barrier treatments that eliminate their food source. By cutting off what spiders eat, they have no reason to stick around. Quarterly treatments keep both ants and spiders under control year-round.
