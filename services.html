Our Pest Control Services

Year-round exterior and interior protection for Blairstown homeowners.

Our Approach

One Monthly Plan. Every Season Covered.

Our monthly plan covers everything your property needs, season by season. In spring and summer we focus on exterior barrier sprays targeting ticks, mosquitoes, wasps, stink bugs, and spotted lanternflies along your perimeter and vegetation. In fall and winter we shift to interior treatments -- rodent monitoring, crack-and-crevice spray, spider control, and overwintering pest management. One flat rate, real service every visit.

What We Treat

Targeted Treatments for Every Pest
