What We Treat

Stink Bugs in Blairstown, NJ

Brown marmorated stink bugs are an invasive species that has become a major nuisance across Warren County. Every fall, they seek warm overwintering sites — and your home's walls, attics, and window frames are their preferred destination.

Stink bugs are active from late summer through fall (August through November). As temperatures drop, they congregate on sun-warmed exterior walls and squeeze through gaps in siding, window frames, door frames, soffits, and foundation cracks to spend the winter inside your home's wall voids.

Once inside, they emerge on warm winter days — showing up on windows, light fixtures, and ceilings. The key to stink bug control is prevention: treating entry points BEFORE they move in, not after they're already behind your walls where no spray can reach them.

How We Treat It

Our Stink Bug Control Process

01
Cypermethrin or Bifenthrin Exterior Spray
We apply cypermethrin or bifenthrin residual spray directly to exterior entry points. These products create a lethal contact barrier that kills stink bugs as they crawl across treated surfaces trying to enter your home. The residual lasts 30+ days per application.
02
Entry Point Treatment
We treat window frames, door frames, soffits, siding gaps, foundation cracks, utility penetrations, and any visible gaps where stink bugs could enter. This is a seal treatment — we spray every crack and crevice on the exterior envelope of your home where bugs squeeze through.
03
Late Summer Through Fall Treatment
Treatments run August through November — before and during the period when stink bugs actively seek overwintering sites. We treat monthly during this window to maintain a continuous barrier as successive waves of bugs arrive at your home's exterior walls.
Safety

Safe for Your Family and Pets

Pet Safety

All treatment is exterior-only, applied to high surfaces like window frames, soffits, and upper foundation walls. Once the spray dries (30-45 minutes), the product is bonded to surfaces and poses no risk to pets in the yard. No interior application is needed for prevention.

Kid Safety

Since treatment targets exterior entry points — soffits, upper window frames, door frames, and siding gaps — children have no contact with treated surfaces during normal play. The product is safe once dry and is applied to areas well above ground level in most cases.

Wildlife Safety

Exterior-only application to man-made surfaces (siding, frames, soffits) means no impact on ground-dwelling beneficial insects or pollinators. We are treating your home's exterior shell — not vegetation, soil, or garden areas. Targeted application with minimal environmental exposure.
