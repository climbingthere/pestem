Keep Stink Bugs Out Before Fall Hits

Professional exterior perimeter treatments that stop stink bugs before they migrate into your home. Targeted crack-and-crevice applications at every entry point, safe for your family and pets.

Treatment Method

Exterior Perimeter Spray -- Every Entry Point Covered

Stink bugs enter homes through gaps you do not even notice -- around windows, door frames, vents, siding gaps, and foundation cracks. Every fall, they migrate from fields and trees to warm structures, looking for a place to overwinter.

We apply a synthetic pyrethroid to all potential entry points on your home's exterior in late summer (August through September), before stink bugs begin their fall migration. This creates a lethal barrier at every crack and crevice they would use to get inside.

  • Synthetic pyrethroid applied to all exterior entry points
  • Windows, door frames, vents, siding gaps, and foundation targeted
  • Applied August through September before fall migration
  • Exterior-only application -- no interior treatments unless needed
  • Crack-and-crevice method minimizes product use
Kid Safety

Safe for Your Children

All spray is applied to exterior surfaces only. There are no interior treatments unless an infestation is already inside your home. Dry time is 30 to 60 minutes, after which all treated surfaces are safe to touch.

Since the application targets cracks, crevices, and high surfaces on the exterior of your home, children have virtually no contact with treated areas during normal play.

Pet Safety

Safe for Dogs and Cats

Keep pets away from exterior surfaces during application and for one hour afterward. Once the product is dry, there is zero risk. We specifically avoid pet doors and areas where your pets frequently rest or contact the exterior walls.

If your dog likes to lie against the foundation or your cat uses a specific window ledge, let us know and we will adjust our application to keep those areas clear.
