Stink Bug Control Blairstown NJ

Stop them before they get inside -- exterior treatment in late summer and fall is the only approach that works.

The Problem

Brown marmorated stink bugs overwinter inside NJ homes. They enter through tiny gaps around windows, doors, soffits, and siding in September-October seeking warmth. Once inside, they're nearly impossible to eliminate without making the house smell.

What We Spray

Deltamethrin or lambda-cyhalothrin applied to exterior -- all window and door frames, soffits, eaves, siding gaps, foundation vents. Creates a contact barrier they can't cross.

Timing Is Everything

August-October. After they're inside, exterior spray does nothing. Early treatment is the only effective approach.

Interior Treatment

Also available: Interior crack and crevice treatment for existing infestations.

Kids

Exterior application only -- no contact risk once dry. Interior crack-and-crevice treatment is applied behind baseboards and in wall voids, not on surfaces children touch.

Pets

Safe once dry. Products are applied to high areas (soffits, eaves, window frames) that pets don't contact.

General Household

No odor, no residue on living surfaces. Treatment targets entry points and structural gaps only.

What NOT to Do

  • Don't crush them indoors -- releases the smell that attracts more
  • Don't use bug bombs -- spreads the odor throughout the house
  • Don't rely on window/door screening alone -- gaps are too small to see
