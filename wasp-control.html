Wasp & Hornet Removal Blairstown NJ

Nests in eaves, decks, and sheds are a sting risk all summer. We remove them safely and treat to prevent rebuilding.

Species We Handle

Yellow jackets (ground and aerial nests), paper wasps (eaves, deck rails), bald-faced hornets (large aerial nests in trees/shrubs), European hornets.

What We Do

Direct nest treatment with dust or aerosol insecticide (depending on location), full nest removal where safely accessible, residual spray around the treated area to prevent re-nesting.

Timing

Active nests May-October. Yellow jackets peak August-September -- most aggressive when nest is largest.

Prevention

Residual spray around eaves, soffits, and overhangs in early spring stops queens from establishing nests in the first place.

During Treatment

We treat in early morning or evening when wasps are least active. Residents should stay inside during treatment and 30 minutes after.

DIY Warning

  • Never block a ground nest entrance -- yellow jackets will find another exit and become extremely aggressive
  • Never spray a large aerial hornet nest at night with a flashlight -- they attack the light source
  • Store-bought sprays rarely penetrate deep enough into a mature nest to kill the queen

What's Included in the Plan

One nest removal per month included. Additional nests same visit: $25/nest.
