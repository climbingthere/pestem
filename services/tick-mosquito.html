Protection When It Counts

Most pest control companies spray on a fixed calendar. We don't. At Pest 'Em, we time every tick and mosquito treatment using Growing Degree Day (GDD) data — the same heat-tracking model university entomologists use to predict exactly when pests emerge in Warren County. That means our barrier sprays land in the narrow window when ticks and mosquitoes are most active and most vulnerable.

Warren County is the front line for tick-borne illness. Black-legged ticks (also called deer ticks) carry Lyme disease, anaplasmosis, and babesiosis, and they're active from early spring through late fall. Mosquitoes bring their own risks — West Nile and Eastern Equine Encephalitis — and one untreated yard can produce thousands of biting adults in a single season.

Our professional exterior barrier spray covers the high-risk zones on your property: tree lines, lawn perimeters, shrub beds, leaf litter edges, and shaded harborage areas where ticks quest and mosquitoes rest. The result is a property your family can actually use again — without the constant worry about a tick check after every walk through the yard.
