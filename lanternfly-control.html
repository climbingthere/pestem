Spotted Lanternfly Is Destroying NJ Trees. We Stop It.

Contact insecticide treatments and egg mass removal to protect your trees and property. Active season coverage from May through November, plus winter egg removal to break the cycle.

Treatment Method

Contact Insecticide and Egg Mass Removal

We apply contact insecticide (bifenthrin or permethrin) directly to tree trunks, stems, and surfaces where spotted lanternflies feed. These insects congregate on bark and lower trunks, making targeted treatment highly effective.

We also target egg masses on trees, rocks, and structures -- physically scraping them off and spraying the surface to prevent re-laying. Repeat applications every 3 to 4 weeks during the active season (May through November) keep populations under control.

Importantly, we do not use systemic insecticides that are absorbed through the soil. Our contact-only approach stays on the surface and breaks down quickly, protecting pollinators and the surrounding ecosystem.

  • Bifenthrin or permethrin contact spray on tree trunks and stems
  • Physical egg mass removal from trees, rocks, and structures
  • Treatments every 3-4 weeks during active season
  • Contact-only -- no systemic soil-absorbed insecticides
  • Safe for pollinators when applied to bark, not flowers
Kid Safety

Safe for Your Children

Treated surfaces (tree bark and lower trunks) should be avoided for 2 hours after application. We do not treat play equipment, swing sets, or active-use areas -- only the trees and surfaces where lanternflies feed.

After the 2-hour window, all treated surfaces are safe. Our technicians clearly mark which trees were treated so you know exactly where the product was applied.

Pet Safety

Safe for Dogs and Cats

The same 2-hour dry window applies to pets. Once the product has dried on tree bark and surfaces, it poses no risk to dogs or cats. We avoid spraying near pet water bowls, feeding areas, and spots where pets rest outdoors.

If your dog has a favorite tree to lie under or your cat frequents a specific area of the yard, let us know and we will work around it.
