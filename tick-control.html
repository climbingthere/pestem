What We Treat

Ticks in Blairstown, NJ

Blairstown sits at the intersection of farmland and forest — prime tick habitat. Deer ticks (black-legged ticks) and lone star ticks thrive in the leaf litter, tall grass, and wooded edges that border most properties in Warren County.

Ticks are active from early spring through late fall, with peak activity from May through July. They don't jump or fly — they crawl up from ground level at the boundary where your mowed lawn meets unmaintained vegetation, brush lines, and wooded areas.

Lyme disease, anaplasmosis, and babesiosis are all transmitted by ticks found in our area. Prevention means treating the zones where ticks wait for hosts — not spraying open lawn where they don't survive.

How We Treat It

Our Tick Control Process

01
Bifenthrin Barrier Spray
We use Bifen IT (bifenthrin 7.9%) as our primary product — a professional-grade synthetic pyrethroid that kills ticks on contact and provides 30-day residual protection. Permethrin is used as a secondary treatment along the yard perimeter for extended knockdown.
02
Targeted Application Zones
We spray a 3-foot barrier at lawn edges, wooded boundaries, shrub beds, and leaf litter areas. We also treat along fence lines, stone walls, and any transition zones between maintained lawn and wild vegetation. We do NOT spray open lawn — ticks don't live there.
03
Monthly, April Through October
Treatments run monthly during tick season — seven visits total. Each visit re-establishes the barrier that breaks down naturally over 30 days. Consistent monthly application prevents population buildup throughout the entire active season.
Safety

Safe for Your Family and Pets

Pet Safety

Bifenthrin is safe for dogs and cats once the spray has dried completely — typically 30 to 45 minutes. We recommend keeping pets inside during application and for the drying period. Once dry, the product bonds to surfaces and is not absorbed through paws.

Kid Safety

Children can return to treated areas once the spray is fully dry (30-45 minutes). The product is applied at lawn edges and wooded borders — not play areas, sandboxes, or open lawn where kids spend time. Targeted application means minimal contact exposure.

Wildlife Safety

Our perimeter-only approach avoids broadcast spraying that would affect beneficial insects on open lawn. We do not treat flowering plants or pollinator gardens. The 3-foot edge treatment targets tick habitat specifically while leaving the rest of your yard untouched.
