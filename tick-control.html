The Risk

Why Tick Control Matters Here

Warren County sits in one of the highest-risk zones for Lyme disease in the entire country. Wooded lots, high deer populations, and humid summers create the perfect storm for blacklegged ticks.

Lyme disease can cause lasting neurological damage, chronic joint pain, and fatigue if not caught early. Prevention is far more effective than treatment -- and far less expensive.

  • NJ reports 3,000+ Lyme cases annually
  • Warren County is classified high-incidence for Lyme
  • Tick season runs March through November
  • Nymphal ticks (hardest to spot) peak May-July
  • Children and pets are at highest risk

Our Approach

How Pestem Tick Control Works

We use a multi-step treatment program timed to the tick lifecycle, targeting ticks when they're most vulnerable throughout the season.

April - May

Early Season Treatment

First application targets overwintering adult ticks and early-stage nymphs. Focus on leaf litter, stone walls, and wood line edges where they emerge.

June - August

Peak Season Protection

Monthly treatments during peak nymph season -- the most dangerous window. Barrier sprays on yard perimeter, play areas, and high-traffic zones.

September - November

Late Season Defense

Final treatments target adult ticks before they lay eggs for next year, breaking the reproduction cycle and reducing next season's population.

Treatment Areas

Where We Treat on Your Property

Ticks don't live in sunny, mowed lawn. They concentrate in specific habitat zones. We target these areas precisely, maximizing effectiveness while using less product.

  • Yard perimeter and woodland edges
  • Stone walls and rock borders
  • Under decks, porches, and sheds
  • Gardens, mulch beds, and ground cover
  • Children's play areas and pet zones
Pet & Family Safe

Safe for Kids and Pets

Every product is selected for its safety profile. Once dry (usually about 30 minutes), treated areas are completely safe for children and pets. We use targeted application methods to minimize environmental impact.

Our technicians walk your property with you, explain exactly what we're applying, and answer any questions about product safety. No surprises, no jargon.
