Why Tick Control Matters in Warren County

Warren County, New Jersey sits in the heart of one of the highest Lyme disease risk areas in the entire United States. The combination of wooded lots, abundant deer populations, and humid summers creates the perfect environment for blacklegged ticks (deer ticks) to thrive.

The Lyme Disease Threat

Lyme disease is transmitted through the bite of an infected blacklegged tick and can cause serious, long-term health problems if not caught early. Symptoms include:

  • Fever, headache, and fatigue that can mimic the flu
  • The characteristic "bull's-eye" rash (though not everyone develops one)
  • Joint pain and swelling, especially in the knees
  • Neurological problems including facial palsy and meningitis
  • Chronic symptoms that can persist for months or years if untreated

New Jersey consistently ranks among the top 5 states for reported Lyme disease cases. Professional tick control is not optional in Warren County; it is essential.

How Pestem Treats Your Property

Our tick control program is built around the science of tick behavior. Ticks do not fly or jump. They wait on the tips of grasses and shrubs in shaded, humid areas along the edges of your yard, trails, and wooded borders. We target these areas specifically.

1
Initial Property Assessment

We walk your entire property to identify high-risk zones: wooded edges, stone walls, ground cover, leaf litter, play areas, and paths where your family and pets spend time.

2
Intensive First Treatment

The initial application is a thorough treatment that knocks down existing tick populations. We spray all perimeter zones, shaded areas, and transition zones between lawn and woods.

3
Recurring Treatments (Every 3-4 Weeks)

From April through October, we return every 3-4 weeks to reapply barrier treatments. This schedule is calibrated to the lifecycle of ticks in our region.

4
Granular Treatments for Shaded Areas

For heavily wooded lots, we supplement our spray program with granular treatments that work from the ground up, reaching ticks where they live.

Pet Safety

We know your pets spend as much time in the yard as your kids do. Every product we use is selected for both effectiveness against ticks and safety around animals. Our treatments are safe for dogs and cats once the application has dried, which typically takes about 30 minutes. We will always let you know when it is safe to let pets back outside.

Our tick treatments actually help protect your pets too. Dogs are highly susceptible to Lyme disease, and a treated yard dramatically reduces their exposure to infected ticks.
