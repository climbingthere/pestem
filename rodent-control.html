The Problem

Why Blairstown Has Serious Rodent Pressure

Warren County properties sit on wooded lots with stone walls, wood piles, and old foundations — ideal harborage for mice and rats. When temperatures drop in October, mice move indoors seeking warmth. A single mouse can squeeze through a gap the width of a pencil. Once inside, they nest in insulation, chew wiring, contaminate food storage, and reproduce fast — one pair can produce 60+ offspring in a year.

Blairstown's mix of rural acreage and older homes means rodent pressure is constant. Without active monitoring and exclusion, mice will find their way in every fall and stay through spring.

A mouse only needs a 1/4-inch gap to enter your home. If you can fit a pencil in it, a mouse can get through it.

Safety

Safe for Your Family and Pets

Pet Safety

Protecta LP bait stations are fully tamper-resistant — dogs and cats physically cannot access the bait inside. The stations are locked and anchored. Interior snap traps are placed exclusively in areas pets cannot reach: behind heavy appliances, inside closed cabinets, and in sealed utility spaces.

Kid Safety

All exterior bait stations require a specialized key to open and are anchored to the ground. Children cannot open or move them. Interior traps are placed in inaccessible locations — behind refrigerators, inside cabinet voids, and in sealed basement utility areas where children do not go.

Wildlife Safety

Protecta LP stations are designed to exclude non-target wildlife. The entry points are sized specifically for mice and rats — squirrels, chipmunks, and birds cannot access the bait. We monitor stations monthly and remove any that are no longer needed to minimize environmental exposure.
