Rodent Prevention Blairstown NJ

Wooded Warren County lots mean constant mouse pressure -- especially in fall. We seal the perimeter before they move in.

The Problem

Field mice, deer mice, and occasionally rats in Blairstown. Wooded lots give them easy access. In fall they seek warmth inside -- foundation gaps, utility penetrations, garage doors. Once inside they nest, chew wiring, and contaminate insulation.

What We Do

  • 1. Exterior perimeter spray and granule bait stations placed at key entry zones
  • 2. Foundation inspection -- identify and document gaps (we note them, exclusion work is homeowner's responsibility unless added as service)
  • 3. Winter interior visits: bait station check and refill, snap trap monitoring if installed

Products

Rodenticide bait blocks in tamper-resistant stations (safe from kids and pets by design). Bifenthrin perimeter spray also deters rodent activity.

Kids & Pets

Bait stations are tamper-resistant -- children and pets cannot access the bait inside. Stations are placed at foundation level out of high-traffic areas.

Signs You Have a Problem

  • Droppings near foundation or in garage corners
  • Gnaw marks on woodwork or siding
  • Nesting material in garage corners or attic
  • Scratching sounds in walls at night

Add-On Available

Interior snap trap installation and monitoring available as add-on service for active infestations.
