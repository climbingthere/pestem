What We Treat

Mosquitoes in Blairstown, NJ

Warren County's mix of streams, wetlands, and shaded properties creates ideal mosquito breeding and harboring conditions. Blairstown homeowners deal with aggressive mosquito populations from late May through September — making evenings outdoors miserable without treatment.

Mosquitoes don't fly far from where they rest during the day. They hide in the undersides of shrubs, dense ground cover, shaded vegetation, and areas near standing water. When evening comes, they emerge from these resting sites to feed — which is why barrier treatment works so effectively.

Asian tiger mosquitoes and common house mosquitoes are both present in our area. Both species rest in vegetation during daylight hours and are active at dawn and dusk, making yard enjoyment difficult without a residual barrier in place.

How We Treat It

Our Mosquito Control Process

01
Bifenthrin Barrier Spray
We apply bifenthrin-based barrier spray to mosquito resting sites. This professional-grade residual insecticide kills mosquitoes on contact when they land on treated surfaces and continues working for up to 30 days between applications.
02
Resting Site Targeting
We treat the undersides of shrubs, dense vegetation, shaded areas under decks and porches, and foliage around standing water sources. These are the places mosquitoes spend 90% of their time — kill them here and they never make it to your evening barbecue.
03
Monthly, May Through September
Five monthly treatments cover the full mosquito season in Blairstown. We apply in early morning when pollinators are less active and mosquitoes are still resting in vegetation. Each treatment provides continuous protection until the next visit.
Safety

Safe for Your Family and Pets

Pet Safety

The barrier spray is safe for dogs and cats once fully dry — typically 30 to 60 minutes depending on humidity. Keep pets inside during application. Once the product dries, it bonds to leaf surfaces and is not transferable to fur or paws during normal yard activity.

Kid Safety

Children can return to the yard once the spray has dried (30-60 minutes). We target vegetation — undersides of leaves, dense shrubs, shaded areas — not open lawn, play structures, or high-traffic areas where kids spend time. Dry product stays on treated surfaces.

Wildlife Safety

We apply in early morning to avoid treating areas where pollinators are actively foraging. We do not spray flowering plants, vegetable gardens, or pollinator beds. Our targeted approach treats mosquito resting sites only — leaving beneficial insects undisturbed in the rest of your landscape.
