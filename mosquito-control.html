Mosquitoes Ruin Outdoor Living

Warren County's wooded landscape, streams, and standing water create perfect mosquito breeding conditions. From June through September, spending time outside after 5pm becomes miserable without proper treatment.

Citronella candles and bug spray aren't cutting it. You invested in a patio, a fire pit, a deck — you should actually be able to use them. Pestem's barrier treatments target mosquitoes where they rest and breed, dramatically reducing populations across your entire property.

Why Barrier Sprays Work

Unlike foggers or zappers that only address active mosquitoes, our barrier spray treats the vegetation where mosquitoes rest during the day — the undersides of leaves, shrubs, and shaded areas. When mosquitoes land on treated surfaces, they're eliminated.

The residual barrier lasts 3-4 weeks, providing continuous protection between visits. Most customers see an 85% reduction in mosquito activity after the first treatment.

How Mosquito Treatment Works

We start by walking your property to identify mosquito resting areas and breeding sites. Common culprits include clogged gutters, bird baths, tree holes, and low-lying areas that hold water.

Our technicians then apply a targeted barrier spray to all vegetation and structures where mosquitoes congregate. We treat the perimeter of your yard, around your patio and deck areas, and along any water features.

Treatments are applied every 3 weeks from May through September. The product is safe for pets and children once dry, typically within 30 minutes.
