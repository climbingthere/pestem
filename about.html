Our Story

Built for Warren County, by Warren County

We live here. We raise our families here. And we got tired of the same pest problems everyone else was dealing with — ticks everywhere, mosquitoes ruining every summer evening, mice finding their way inside every fall.

The options were either expensive national chains who didn't know our terrain, or one-time spray-and-pray services that never lasted. So we built Pest 'Em — affordable monthly plans that actually prevent problems before they start.

We use low-toxicity, EPA-registered products that are safe for your family and pets once dry (typically 30-60 minutes). We know the wooded lots, the deer trails, the standing water spots that make Warren County a hotspot for ticks and mosquitoes. And we treat accordingly.
