About Pestem

Locally owned pest control built on prevention, safety, and Warren County community values.

Our Story

Built for Warren County

Pestem started with a simple idea: homeowners in Warren County deserve pest control that actually prevents problems instead of just reacting to them.

Too many national companies send technicians who don't know the local terrain, the seasonal pressures, or the specific pests that thrive in northwestern New Jersey. We're different. We live here. We raise our families here.

We know that the wooded lots in Blairstown face different pest pressures than the suburban neighborhoods in Hackettstown. We know tick season starts earlier than most people think, and that stink bugs start scouting for winter shelter in late August. That local knowledge shapes everything we do.

Our Mission

Prevention Over Reaction

Most pest control companies make their money on emergency calls. We'd rather you never need one.

Our prevention-first approach uses scheduled barrier treatments, entry-point sealing, and proactive monitoring to stop pest problems before they start. The result: fewer surprises, lower long-term costs, and a home you can enjoy year-round.

Every product we use is selected for two things: effectiveness and safety. We're parents and pet owners ourselves. We would never apply anything to your property that we wouldn't use on our own.
