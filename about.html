About Pest 'Em

Local pest control built on honesty -- not billing cycles.

The Terminix Backstory

Three years as a top specialist. Left because charging homeowners for mosquito spray in January isn't pest control -- it's billing for billing's sake.

Every month, same truck, same spray, same invoice -- regardless of whether mosquitoes were active, whether the product was effective in 30-degree weather, or whether the homeowner was actually getting any value. The model was designed around revenue, not results.

Pest 'Em was built to fix that.

The Model

4 seasons, real service every visit. The treatment changes with the weather because pest pressure changes with the weather.

Exterior when it matters: April through October, monthly barrier spray targeting ticks, mosquitoes, wasps, stink bugs, and lanternflies along your perimeter and vegetation.

Interior when it's cold: November through March, monthly interior visits -- rodent bait stations, crack-and-crevice spray, spider control, foundation inspection.

Same flat monthly rate year-round. Same tech on every visit. If pests show up between treatments, we come back free.

Based in Blairstown

We live here. We serve the community we live in. Our routes are built around Warren County -- Blairstown first, then extending to Hackettstown, Hope, Belvidere, Columbia, and surrounding towns. When you call, you get someone who knows the area, knows the pest pressure, and shows up on time.

100% satisfaction guarantee. Free re-treatments between visits. If you're not happy, we make it right -- no questions, no runaround.
