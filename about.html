Our Story

Built in Warren County, For Warren County

Pestem started with a simple frustration: every pest control company we called was either a big national franchise that didn't understand our area, or a one-man operation that couldn't keep a consistent schedule. We knew Warren County deserved better.

We grew up hiking the trails around Blairstown, fishing in the Paulinskill, and dealing with the same tick and mosquito problems every family here faces. When our own dogs kept coming home covered in ticks despite expensive treatments from big-name companies, we decided to do something about it.

Pestem was built on the idea that pest control should be simple, transparent, and actually work. No upselling, no scare tactics, no hidden fees. Just honest, effective treatment from people who live here and care about this community.

Pet-Safe Promise

Your Pets Are Family. We Treat Them That Way.

Every product we use is EPA-registered and selected specifically for safety around pets and children. Our technicians are trained to apply treatments in a way that minimizes any contact risk.

After every treatment, we'll tell you exactly when it's safe for pets to return to the yard — typically 30 minutes once the application has dried. For indoor treatments, we use targeted gel baits and crack-and-crevice applications that keep products away from living areas.

Many of our customers chose Pestem specifically because we prioritize pet safety. We take that responsibility seriously.
